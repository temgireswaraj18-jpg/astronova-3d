Privacy Policy for Astronova 3D: Solar System & Kepler Lab

Effective Date: November 2026

1. No User Login or Personal Account Collection

Astronova 3D operates 100% locally on your device. We do not require user registration, login, email addresses, names, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (PII). All simulation preferences, quiz scores, and Pro unlock states are saved strictly locally on your device.

2. Hardware Sensor Usage (2D/3D Presence Mode)

Astronova 3D uses your device's standard orientation/accelerometer sensor solely in real time to switch smoothly between the 3D Perspective View (when held in hand) and the 2D Top-Down Overview Map (when resting flat). No sensor data is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Astronova 3D displays non-intrusive banners and optional rewarded video advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID), coarse network information, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure ads in compliance with Google Play policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android Settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more at Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is an educational astronomy and physics simulation tool suitable for general audiences and students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the official Google Play Store listing page.
